## 2. SUPPLY CHAIN LOGISTICS, TIMELINES & SHIPPING TIERS
* **Geographic Fulfillment Limitation:** Nuit focuses operations strictly within the domestic Egyptian market. The brand does **not** process, fulfill, or ship orders internationally outside of Egypt.
* **In-House Processing Latency:** Orders require a baseline window of 1 to 2 business days for automated inventory picking, secondary quality control inspection, secure product heat-sealing, and digital logistics manifest generation.
* **Domestic Logistics Delivery Duration:** Deliveries are executed within a strict 3 to 5 business days transit window across national shipping partners.
* **Bound Flat Shipping Fee:** EGP 80.00 flat across all Egyptian Governorates, without geographic exception.
* **Free Shipping Incentive Threshold:** Automatically and systematically triggered on any single checkout session carrying a net shopping cart value exceeding EGP 1,500.00.

## 3. STRICT EXCHANGE & REFUND POLICY (OFFICIAL WEBSITE STANDARD)
To maintain strict fairness, hygienic standards, and high cosmetic quality thresholds across batches, the brand enforces unyielding parameters regarding transaction reversals.

### 3.1 Defective or Damaged Products ONLY Mandate
* Returns or exchanges for reasons unrelated to physical manufacturing damage or shipping defects (such as a change of mind, personal blind-buy dissatisfaction, or scent preference shifts) are **NOT permitted** under any circumstances. Scent is highly personal, and things don't always go as planned. Customers are explicitly invited to message the care team over Instagram or WhatsApp DM to discuss notes and get support *before* placing an order if they are unsure about a scent.

### 3.2 The 14-Day Strict Reporting Window
* Customers possess an absolute timeline of **14 calendar days** from the verified timestamp of receiving the item to submit an eligible return or exchange request. Any request transmitted after this 14-day window expires will be automatically rejected without exception, even if the product contains a verified physical defect.

### 3.3 Absolute Disqualification Parameters (Strictly Non-Refundable & Non-Exchangeable)
The following product classes and purchase states are barred from return, exchange, or financial remediation tracks under any circumstances:
* **Products with Samples Available:** If Nuit offers a purchasable trial sample format of a specific fragrance on the store, the full-size version is entirely ineligible for exchange or refund. This restriction remains binding even if the sample format is temporarily out of stock or unavailable at the time of the request. Nuit strongly recommends purchasing a sample first to ensure the scent matches personal preferences before buying full-sized products.
* **Discounted & Campaign Items:** Products purchased utilizing any discount codes, bundle offers, seasonal promotions, flash sales, vouchers, or any form of special tiered pricing are classified as final sale and are strictly ineligible for exchange or refund.
* **Seasonal or Limited Edition Products:** Any item labeled or marketed as seasonal, limited edition, archive stock, or part of a special collection/collaborative drop (e.g., *Avec Hatshepsut*) is non-returnable and non-exchangeable.
* **Khamrias & Perfume Oils:** For strict hygienic, sanitary, and public health safety reasons, all Khamrias and concentrated perfume oils ("Shots") are completely non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

### 3.4 Verified Damaged or Defective Item Subroutine
* **Eligible Criteria:** Product arrives clearly damaged due to manufacturing errors or shipping transit, such as major fluid leakage, broken glass flacons, or complete structural atomizer pump failures.
* **Minor Variations Note:** Minor leaks, faint scuffs, or cosmetic packaging imperfections may naturally occur due to temperature fluctuations or carrier handling during transit and are not always considered valid grounds for a return. Nuit reserves the right to assess the situation and determine absolute eligibility.
* **Verification Assets & Process:** The customer must contact support via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp DM within the mandatory 14-day window. The customer must provide their system order number alongside a brief description and clear photos and/or videos documenting the explicit issue.

### 3.5 Fees & Financial Resolutions
* **Approved Return Processing Fees:** If an exchange or refund is formally approved due to verified product damage or manufacturing defects, there are **no extra fees** or restocking charges applied to the customer. Nuit handles the fulfillment correction entirely at zero added fee.
* **Shipping Fee Non-Refundability:** Original baseline shipping fees paid by the client during the initial checkout sequence are entirely non-refundable.
* **Refund Processing Latency:** Approved monetary refunds are systematically processed via the original method of payment within **14 business days** after the central fulfillment facility receives, inspects, and validates the physical state of the returned item.
