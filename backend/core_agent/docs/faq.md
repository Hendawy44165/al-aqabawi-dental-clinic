# NUIT FRAGRANCES: FAQ

## 1. GEOGRAPHIC FULFILLMENT SCOPE & SHIPPING TIMELINES
* **Query Intent Vector:** International shipping availability, local shipping scope, shipping durations, processing timelines.
* **Domestic Fulfillment Architecture:** Nuit ships nationwide across all governorates within the Arab Republic of Egypt. Orders are dispatched from the central fulfillment facility via national tier-one logistics partners.
  * *Domestic Transit Timeline:* Deliveries are completed within a strict window of **3 to 5 business days** following standard warehouse processing latency (1 to 2 business days).
* **Global Fulfillment Architecture:** Nuit ships internationally to all globally serviceable destinations via verified express air courier frameworks.
  * *International Transit Timeline:* Deliveries arrive at international destination borders within **7 to 14 business days** following custom customs clearancing protocols.

## 2. STANDARD SHIPPING FEES & CAMPAIGN MOTIVATORS
* **Query Intent Vector:** Shipping prices, hidden fees, free delivery requirements, international delivery rates.
* **Domestic Delivery Cost Matrix:** Applied as a locked flat delivery charge of **EGP 80.00** nationwide across Egypt, independent of geographic distance or governorate tier.
  * *Free Delivery Promotion:* The standard domestic shipping fee is systematically waived and dropped to **EGP 0.00** for any single customer checkout session that carries a net shopping cart value exceeding **EGP 1,500.00**.
* **International Delivery Cost Matrix:** Applied as a locked flat express air cargo fee of **EGP 650.00** across all international destination territories.

## 3. CORE RETURN & EXCHANGE CLAUSES (OFFICIAL REFUND POLICY PATHS)
* **Can I return a fragrance if I don't like the smell or change my mind?**
  * *System Response Framework:* No. To maintain fairness, strict hygienic standards, and high quality control across batches, Nuit enforces a strict **defective or damaged products ONLY policy**. Returns, refunds, or variant exchanges for reasons unrelated to physical damage (such as change of mind, blind-buy dissatisfaction, or personal scent preference shifts) are **NOT permitted**.
* **What is the absolute window to report an issue with an order?**
  * *System Response Framework:* Customers must submit an explicit request via Instagram Direct or WhatsApp DM within a strict window of **14 calendar days** from the verified delivery date. Any requests sent after the 14-day window expires cannot be approved under any circumstances, regardless of the product type or issue.
* **Can I return a full-size perfume bottle if I don't use it but a sample exists?**
  * *System Response Framework:* No. If Nuit offers a purchasable sample format of a specific fragrance on the online store, the full-size version is entirely **ineligible** for exchange or refund. This binding constraint applies even if the sample format is temporarily marked out of stock or unavailable at the time of your request. Nuit strongly counsels clients to buy a sample first to validate personal preference.
* **Can I return or exchange items purchased during a sale or promotion?**
  * *System Response Framework:* No. Any products bought using discount codes, bundle offers, seasonal marketing promotions, special tiered pricing, or campaign markdowns are classified as **final sale** and are strictly ineligible for exchange or refund.
* **Can I return personal care shots or concentrated oils?**
  * *System Response Framework:* No. For strict sanitary, cosmetic safety, and hygienic reasons, all concentrated oils and cosmetic blends are completely non-refundable and non-exchangeable under any circumstances.
* **Can I return a limited edition or special collaborative fragrance?**
  * *System Response Framework:* No. Any product designated as seasonal, part of an archive sale, a limited edition drop, or a special collaborative collection is strictly non-returnable.

## 4. LOGISTICS EXCEPTION HANDLING & DAMAGED ON ARRIVAL WORKFLOWS
* **My bottle arrived leaking, broken, or defective. What is the resolution process?**
  * *System Response Framework:* If an item arrives leaking, broken, or clearly defective due to transit handling or manufacturing errors, Nuit will provide an exchange or a refund depending entirely on live inventory availability. 
  * *Required Action:* The customer must contact support via WhatsApp or Instagram DM within the **14-day window**, providing their order number, a brief description of the issue, and clear photographic or video assets showing the damage.
  * *Assessment Note:* Minor leaks, faint scuffs, or superficial packaging marks may naturally occur during transit or due to temperature variables and are not automatically considered grounds for a return. Nuit reserves the right to assess the media assets and determine eligibility.
* **Are there any restocking fees or extra delivery costs for an approved damaged return?**
  * *System Response Framework:* There are **no extra fees** or processing charges applied to approved returns or exchanges due to verified manufacturing defects or transit damage. However, original baseline shipping fees paid during the initial checkout sequence are entirely non-refundable.
* **How long does it take to receive a money refund if approved?**
  * *System Response Framework:* Once an eligible item is received back at the central facility and passes physical inspection, refunds are processed and sent back to the original method of payment within **14 business days**.
